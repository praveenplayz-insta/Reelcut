How it works

On your device

  • The AI models download once, then work from your browser’s cache
  • Your video is never uploaded
  • Exporting runs in real time, so a 30-second clip takes about 30 seconds

Good to know

  • Works best in recent Chrome or Edge, and on a computer
  • Videos over about 20 minutes may be too big for the browser. Trim them first
  • Captions can be wrong. Check the preview before you export

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. It runs on your device, so there is nothing to charge for.

How good are the captions?

Good for clear speech in English and other major languages. Pick “Better” or “Best” for accents or other languages.

Why is the first run slow?

The caption and face models download the first time. After that they load from your browser’s cache.

Which file do I get?

MP4 in recent Chrome, Edge and Safari. Older browsers make WebM.
